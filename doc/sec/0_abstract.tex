\abstract{%
Document intelligence connects page perception with reasoning over text, layout, tables and images. Its central challenge is preserving the evidence needed for an answer across recognition, representation, retrieval and computation. This survey compares OCR-based, direct-visual and hybrid approaches through five questions: what representation is used, how much context is accessed, which evidence units are retained, how inference is controlled, and what the evaluation verifies. Visual worked examples connect these choices to concrete errors, including lost headers, missing cross-page corrections, insufficient image resolution and unsupported citations. We organize representative methods by their mechanisms rather than list papers chronologically, and distinguish answer correctness, evidence localization, source support and execution. The resulting synthesis guides the selection of document-QA workflows under explicit input and resource constraints.
}
